# B2B Marketplace Platform

A B2B marketplace combining product discovery, ordering, and inventory management with AI-powered summaries, stock suggestions, and recognition of stock quantities at customer locations.

## Overview

OpenMarket connects product discovery and ordering with inventory management and external service integrations. AI capabilities summarize stock information, provide stock-related suggestions, and recognize stock quantities at customer locations to support inventory planning and ordering decisions. The platform combines a Vue and Nuxt.js web application with Kotlin backend services, using Keycloak for authentication and authorization, Liquibase for database schema migrations, and Redis as part of the backend infrastructure.

Docker supports service containerization, while Kubernetes and Helm charts support deployment and configuration. GitHub Packages provides package distribution within the development workflow.

My responsibilities included technical and product ownership, architectural decision-making, and team leadership. Guiding architectural choices and implementation priorities across the platform. I evaluated trade-offs between business requirements, maintainability, and integration needs to inform the technical direction.
