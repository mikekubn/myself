# Financial Platform Frontend Modernization

Frontend engineering leadership and architectural modernization of enterprise financial platforms at Akcenta (RBI Group), combining team leadership, technical decision-making, and hands-on development.

## Overview

As Head of Frontend Development at Akcenta (RBI Group), I led frontend engineering and the architectural modernization of enterprise financial platforms. My work combined technical leadership, hands-on development, and cross-functional collaboration to guide the evolution of the frontend applications.

I led the frontend team and took responsibility for technical decisions, helping translate business requirements into implementation priorities and architectural direction. The role focused on advancing frontend modernization while supporting maintainable application development and consistent engineering practices.

I reported to the directors, communicating development progress, technical priorities, and key decisions to connect engineering work with business objectives.
